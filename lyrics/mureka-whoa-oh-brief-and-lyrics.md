# Party on the Internet (Whoa-Oh)

```text
Bright cheeky early-2010s radio dance-pop, 120 BPM. Four-on-the-floor kick, springy synth bass, handclaps on beats 2 and 4, staccato sawtooth synth answers, digital-message chirps as percussion. Male tenor lead, sung not rapped, middle register, animated, articulate, conspiratorial. Radio-pop form: bouncy stripped-back verse, pre-chorus with pads, snare-roll build and group "like yeah", then the chorus. Chorus: massive, communal, euphoric anthem, heavy kick, loud clap, a supersaw synth lead playing the hook, and a mixed-gender group singing in unison with the tenor, thick, festive, cheering. The crowd answers each title line with a wordless "whoa-oh-oh" hook. Pitch: ingratiating, beat pulled back to one synth pluck. At "I saw the keys" the beat cuts out, lead near-spoken. Final chorus hollow: one kick, one lonely clap, thin distant voices, then stop.
```
